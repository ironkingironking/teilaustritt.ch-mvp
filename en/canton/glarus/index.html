Leaving the church in the canton of Glarus

In the canton of Glarus, you declare in writing that you are leaving the Roman Catholic cantonal church and church municipality (partial withdrawal) to the church municipality where you live. teilaustritt.ch finds the responsible office and creates the letter for free, as PDF or Word.

Create your letter

What applies in the canton of Glarus

  • In writing and signed by hand, preferably by registered mail or A-Post Plus.
  • Your signature does not need to be officially certified.
  • If you leave during the year, you no longer owe church tax for the whole year in which you leave.

Cantonal church body: Katholische Landeskirche Kanton Glarus, Burgstrasse 36, 8750 Glarus
