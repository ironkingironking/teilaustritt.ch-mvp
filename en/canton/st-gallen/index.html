Leaving the church in the canton of St. Gallen

In the canton of St. Gallen, you declare in writing that you are leaving the Roman Catholic cantonal church and church municipality (partial withdrawal) to the church municipality where you live. teilaustritt.ch finds the responsible office and creates the letter for free, as PDF or Word.

Create your letter

What applies in the canton of St. Gallen

  • In writing and signed by hand, preferably by registered mail or A-Post Plus.
  • The signature must be officially certified.
  • Church tax liability ends as soon as your declaration arrives.
  • If you pay withholding tax, request the church tax refund after leaving by 31 March of the following year.

Cantonal church body: Katholischer Konfessionsteil St.Gallen, Klosterhof 6a, 9000 St.Gallen
