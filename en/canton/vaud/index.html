Leaving the church in the canton of Vaud

The canton of Vaud has no church tax; the canton and municipalities fund the recognised churches from general taxes. After sending a 'déclaration de sortie' to the parish council, you can ask your municipality to refund the small share of municipal tax used for worship.

What applies in the canton of Vaud

  • There is no church tax; the churches are funded from general taxes. If you do not belong to a recognised church, you can reclaim the church share of the municipal tax, but not of the cantonal tax. You have to ask again for each tax period.
