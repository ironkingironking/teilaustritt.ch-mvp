Leaving the church in the canton of Basel-Landschaft

In the canton of Basel-Landschaft, you declare in writing that you are leaving the Roman Catholic cantonal church and church municipality (partial withdrawal) to the church municipality where you live. teilaustritt.ch finds the responsible office and creates the letter for free, as PDF or Word.

Create your letter

What applies in the canton of Basel-Landschaft

  • In writing and signed by hand, preferably by registered mail or A-Post Plus.
  • Your signature does not need to be officially certified.
  • If you leave during the year, you no longer owe church tax for the whole year in which you leave.
  • If you do not belong to a recognised church, you can reclaim from your municipality the per-capita share of its contribution to the church municipalities.

Cantonal church body: Röm.-kath. Landeskirche BL, Munzachstrasse 2, Postfach, 4410 Liestal
