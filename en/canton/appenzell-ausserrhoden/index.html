Leaving the church in the canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden

In the canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden, you declare in writing that you are leaving the Roman Catholic cantonal church and church municipality (partial withdrawal) to the church municipality where you live. teilaustritt.ch finds the responsible office and creates the letter for free, as PDF or Word.

Create your letter

What applies in the canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden

  • In writing and signed by hand, preferably by registered mail or A-Post Plus.
  • The signature must be officially certified.
  • Church tax liability ends from the month after your declaration arrives.

Cantonal church body: Katholische Kirche Appenzell Ausserrhoden
