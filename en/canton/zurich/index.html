Leaving the church in the canton of Zurich

In the canton of Zurich, you declare in writing that you are leaving the Roman Catholic cantonal church and church municipality (partial withdrawal) to the church municipality where you live. teilaustritt.ch finds the responsible office and creates the letter for free, as PDF or Word.

Create your letter

What applies in the canton of Zurich

  • In writing and signed by hand, preferably by registered mail or A-Post Plus.
  • Your signature does not need to be officially certified.
  • Church tax liability ends as soon as your declaration arrives.

Cantonal church body: Katholische Kirche im Kanton Zürich, Synodalrat, Hirschengraben 66, 8001 Zürich
