Leaving the church in the canton of Graubünden

In the canton of Graubünden, you declare in writing that you are leaving the Roman Catholic cantonal church and church municipality (partial withdrawal) to the church municipality where you live. teilaustritt.ch finds the responsible office and creates the letter for free, as PDF or Word.

Create your letter

What applies in the canton of Graubünden

  • In writing and signed by hand, preferably by registered mail or A-Post Plus.
  • Your signature does not need to be officially certified.
  • If you leave during the year, you no longer owe church tax for the whole year in which you leave.
  • Cantonal law expressly provides for refunds of church tax collected wrongly.

Cantonal church body: Katholische Landeskirche Graubünden, Geschäftsstelle, Quaderstrasse 11, 7000 Chur
