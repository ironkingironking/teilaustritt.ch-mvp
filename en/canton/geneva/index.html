Leaving the church in the canton of Geneva

Geneva has no public-law church membership and no church tax; church and state are separate. The voluntary religious contribution (CRV) is an optional donation, so no exit letter is needed for tax purposes.

What applies in the canton of Geneva

  • Church tax is invoiced, but payment is voluntary.
